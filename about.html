Meet Paige

First, and most important, congratulations. You are entering such a sweet and exciting phase in life. Planning a wedding on your own can be stressful, but it does not have to be.

This is where I come in. Having someone on your side who knows the ins and outs of planning and executing your big day can truly make or break your wedding experience. I have had the blessing of planning and coordinating hundreds of beautiful weddings, and I have found a true love in helping my brides achieve their dream day and enjoying every step along the way.

Whether it is day of coordination or we are in it together with full planning, I want to be your wedding wizard and walk through each step with you.

xx Paige

Mission statement

I want all my brides to feel like they have a best friend they can turn to in planning their wedding. Someone knowledgeable, who understands what they are going through, and has their best interests at heart.

I want couples to enjoy their engagement and wedding, and for it to never feel like a chore to get married.

How we work together

From first email to last dance

  1. Say hello

    You send your name, your number, and the package you are drawn to. I come back to you with availability and we find a time to talk.

  2. Make a plan

    We settle on the package that actually fits your day, then start building the timeline, the budget, and the vendor list around it.

  3. Design and confirm

    Collaborative layout and design planning with a venue walk through, followed by comprehensive final confirmations with every vendor.

  4. Be fully present

    Rehearsal coordination and full wedding day execution. Every moment is handled with care, and you get to actually be in your own wedding.
