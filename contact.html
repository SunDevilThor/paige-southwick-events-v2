Before you write

A few quick answers

I do not know which package I need yet.

That is completely fine. Leave the package field as it is and tell me where you are in planning. Helping you work that out is part of the job.

I do not have a date or venue yet.

Still worth reaching out. Put a season or a rough month in the date field and we will go from there. Full Service Planning is built for exactly this stage.

Does this form send my details anywhere?

No. Everything stays in your browser. The button simply opens a draft email in your own mail app, addressed to me, with what you typed already written out. Nothing is sent until you send it.
