Good to know

Questions couples ask

Which package is right for me?

If you are newly engaged and want a guide from the very beginning, that is Full Service. If you have a vision and some vendors but want help finishing strong, that is Partial Planning. If everything is booked and you simply want to hand it off and enjoy the day, that is Day Of Coordination. If you are not sure, email me and we will figure it out together.

When should I reach out?

As early as you like. Full Service begins with the ring, so the sooner the better. Partial Planning moves into full swing ninety days before the wedding and Day Of Coordination sixty days before, so please reach out well ahead of those windows to be sure your date is open.

What should I put in my first email?

Your name, your number, and the package you are interested in. Your wedding date and venue are helpful too if you have them. The inquiry page will put all of that together for you.

Can you work with vendors I have already booked?

Yes. Every package includes comprehensive final confirmations with your vendors, including the ones you booked before we started working together.

Is my date available?

Send me your date and venue and I will let you know right away.
